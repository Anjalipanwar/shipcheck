ShipCheck

A QA engineer's pre-launch checkup for your vibe-coded app — in one command.

You built an app with Lovable, Bolt, v0, Replit, Cursor or Claude Code and you are about to share the link. ShipCheck opens it in a real browser, uses it like a first-time visitor — signs up, logs in, tries the main feature, fills in forms, on desktop and on a phone — and writes a report in plain English: what is broken, the proof, and a prompt you can paste into your AI builder to fix it. 

npx @anjalipanwar/shipcheck https://myapp.lovable.app

See a sample report Quickstart (5 minutes) Source on GitHub

What you get

  • A clear verdict, and a table of what was tested and what could not be tested.
  • Every problem with evidence: screenshots, steps to reproduce, what the app answered.
  • A fix prompt for each problem, ready to paste into your AI builder.
  • No finding without evidence. When ShipCheck is not sure, it says “Not tested”.

What it will never do

  • Leave your app, pay for anything, or delete, send or publish something.
  • Attack your app. It only types the kind of messy input real people type.
  • Write a password or your AI key into a report or a file.

Free and yours

ShipCheck is open source (MIT) and runs on your own computer. You bring your own AI key — a free one from Google is enough. Only test apps you own or have permission to test.

ShipCheck is an automated pre-launch check. It is not a security audit and not a guarantee that an app is free of bugs. The sample report was made from a pretend app with planted bugs, with the automatic checks only.
